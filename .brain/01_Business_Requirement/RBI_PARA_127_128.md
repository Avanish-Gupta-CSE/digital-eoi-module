## PART C – OTHER TYPES OF LENDING AND RISK TRANSFER ARRANGEMENTS

### Chapter I – Lending under Consortium / Multiple Banking Arrangements

**127.** The banks may evolve appropriate mechanism for adopting sole bank, multiple bank,
consortium or syndication approach, by framing necessary ground rules on operational aspects.

**128.** Banks shall strengthen its information back-up about the borrowers enjoying credit
facilities from multiple lenders as under:

**(1)** At the time of granting fresh facilities, bank shall obtain declaration from the borrowers
about the credit facilities already enjoyed by them from other lenders in the format specified in
**Annex I**. In the case of existing banks, all the banks shall seek a declaration from their
existing borrowers availing sanctioned limits of **rupees five crore and above** or wherever, it is
in their knowledge that their borrowers are availing credit facilities from other lenders and
introduce a system of exchange of information with other lenders as indicated above.

**(2)** Subsequently, banks shall exchange information about the conduct of the borrowers'
accounts with other lenders (participating REs under consortium/ Multiple Banking
Arrangements) in the format given in **Annex II** at least at **quarterly intervals**.

**(3)** The bank shall obtain regular certification by a professional, preferably a Company
Secretary / Cost Accountant / Chartered Accountant regarding compliance of various statutory
prescriptions that are in vogue as per the format specified in **Annex III**.

**(4)** Further, the bank shall make greater use of credit reports available from Credit
Information Companies listed under Reserve Bank of India (Credit Information Companies)
Directions, 2025.

**(5)** The bank shall also incorporate suitable clauses in the loan agreements in future (at the
time of next renewal in the case of existing facilities) regarding exchange of credit information
so as to address confidentiality issues.

**129.** Further, the consortium leader / bank having the largest exposure shall have the lead role
in monitoring the unhedged foreign exchange exposure of entities.

## The three annexes referenced by para 128

### Annex I (pp. 43–45)
*Minimum Information to be Declared by Borrowing Entities to Banks while Approaching for
Finance under Multiple Banking Arrangement*

- **A. Details of borrowing arrangements from other banks** (institution-wise, facility-wise):
  I. Name & address of bank/institution; II. Facilities availed — (A) fund-based
  (WC/demand/term/short-term/FC loan, corporate loan, line of credit, channel financing, bill
  discounting), (B) non-fund-based other than derivatives (L/C, BG, DPG I&F), (C) derivatives
  contracts (nature, maturity, amount, purpose); III. Date of sanction; IV. Present outstanding
  (for derivatives: negative MTM not due for settlement); V. Overdues (for derivatives: negative
  MTM payable but unpaid); VI. Repayment terms; VII. Security offered (primary, collateral,
  project cash flows, personal/corporate guarantee); VIII. Requests for facilities under process.
- **B. Miscellaneous details (₹ crore)**: CPs raised/outstanding; financing outside banking
  system; unhedged FX exposure (short-term & long-term long/short/net positions, overall net
  position per currency and across currencies); main & allied activities with locations; territory
  of sales & market share; financials incl. DSCR projections and financial covenants agreed with
  other lenders; CIDA accounts; statutory demands; pending litigations; **a declaration
  authorising the bank to share information with other financing banks**.

### Annex II (pp. 46–52)
*Format for Credit Information Exchange* — this is the enclosure in `BRD.pdf` (pages 3–9 of the
scan). Parts I–VI, with Part VI graded Good / Satisfactory / Below Par against the rubric on
pp. 50–52. Verified byte-for-byte consistent with the BRD scan.

### Annex III (p. 53 onward)
*Part I – Diligence Report* addressed to "The Manager, ______ (Name of the Bank)", a
professional's certificate on statutory compliance (Companies Act 2013, MOA/AOA, listing
agreement) for the half year ended.

## Gap analysis vs the UCO Bank BRD letter

The BRD letter (Ref HO/FCC/71/2026-27 dated 21-08-2026) asks for 4 functionalities and attaches
only **Annex II**. Para 128 imposes more than that:

| Obligation | In BRD scope? |
|---|---|
| 128(1) borrower declaration capture in **Annex I** format, incl. ₹5 cr threshold rule | No — not mentioned |
| 128(2) Annex II exchange at least quarterly | Yes — core of the BRD |
| 128(3) **Annex III** professional certification workflow | No — not mentioned |
| 128(4) use of CIC credit reports | No — not mentioned |
| 128(5) confidentiality clause in loan agreements | No — but explains the audit-trail / maker-checker ask |
| 129 lead-bank monitoring of unhedged FX exposure | Implicitly — Annex II Part V carries the currency positions |

Para 127 is enabling text only ("may evolve appropriate mechanism"); it creates no new
software obligation. All binding requirements live in para 128.

Open questions for the business owner:
1. Is Annex I capture in scope for v1, or is it handled by the existing loan appraisal flow?
2. Is Annex III (Diligence Report) tracked in this module or by the concurrent-audit/compliance team?
3. Does the ₹5 crore threshold drive the module's borrower master population?
4. Is "at least quarterly" to be implemented as a fixed calendar (30 Jun / 30 Sep / 31 Dec / 31 Mar)?
