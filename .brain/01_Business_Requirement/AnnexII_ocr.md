**Read this before trusting it**

- Layout artefacts are expected: Roman numerals and cell text are often separated, and tables
  interleave columns. `p. N` refers to the BRD scan page, not the RBI document.
- Known OCR noise: `Annex !!` = "Annex II", `Part - 1` = "Part – I", `Detalls` = "Details",
  `Ill`/`Il`/`11l` = "III", `VIll:` = "VIII".
- Page 9 is the tail of the Part VI rubric and OCR'd poorly. Verify anything from Part VI against
  the PDF before relying on it.
- This file is a *working copy*, not the specification. The RBI direction (Annex II, pp. 46–52)
  is the authority; if this text and the direction disagree, the direction wins.
